# Planet — "Planet to Build and Operate Advanced Space Platform for Google's Project Suncatcher Moonshot"

## Reported (what the source says)
- Planet will **build and operate** two prototype satellites for Google, **targeting launch by early 2027**.
- Mission goal: test the viability and performance of Google TPUs in space, and "the ability of two such spacecraft to work in concert, flying in tandem with high bandwidth cross link communications."
- Satellites use **the same bus as Planet's recently announced Owl mission**.
- No contract value disclosed; no quotes from Will Marshall in the post body.
- The Oct 2026 single-satellite prototype (Transporter-18) is **not** mentioned — it was added later and flew on a "standard Planet platform" (per TechCrunch note); the two-satellite laser-link mission remains the 2027 milestone.

## Relevance to the Entrant
- Planet is positioning its bus as a **hosted-compute platform** for a hyperscaler. For the Entrant this is both a validation (EO buses can host serious compute) and a competitive signal (the incumbent EO operator may bundle in-orbit processing before a startup can).
- The Owl-class bus is the reference hardware class for "refrigerator-sized, ~1 kW, 4 accelerators" — a useful sizing anchor for the Entrant's own payload envelope.
