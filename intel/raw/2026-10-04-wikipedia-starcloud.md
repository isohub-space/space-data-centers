# Wikipedia — Starcloud (orbital data-centre startup) — facts as of Oct 2026

## Reported (what the source says)
- Founded Jan 2024 (as Lumen Orbit), El Segundo → Redmond, WA.
- Funding: seed ~**$34M** (by Mar 2025); **Series A $170M at $1.1B** (2026-03-30); **Series B $250M at $2.3B** (Aug 2026) with **NVIDIA $25M**, Cisco, Benchmark; earlier Sequoia, a16z scout, **In-Q-Tel**.
- **Starcloud-1** launched **2025-11-02** (Falcon 9) with an **NVIDIA H100**; first LLM inference and in-orbit training on a data-centre-class GPU (Dec 2025).
- **Starcloud-2** (announced 2026-03-07) "intended to be the first to mine Bitcoin in space"; **Starlink mini-lasers** to be integrated on the next **25** satellites (2026-05-26).
- FCC proposal (2026-02-03) for **up to 88,000** satellites. Crusoe Energy agreement for late-2026 deployment. 2024: DIU Accelerator member.
- (IEEE Spectrum, Jul 2026: Starcloud-1's radiator "was too weak to let the chip run at full power.")
