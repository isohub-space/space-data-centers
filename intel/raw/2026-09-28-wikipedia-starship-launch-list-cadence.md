# Wikipedia — Starship launch list (cadence and payload mass, 2025–2026)

## Reported (what the source says)
- **2025 (5 flights, Block 2):**
  - F7 Jan 16 — 10 Starlink simulators (~20,000 kg) — ship lost
  - F8 Mar 6 — 4 simulators (~8,000 kg) — ship lost
  - F9 May 27 — 8 simulators (~16,000 kg) — booster & ship lost
  - F10 Aug 26 — 8 simulators (~16,000 kg) — success
  - F11 Oct 13 — 8 simulators (~16,000 kg) — success
- **2026 (3 flights to date, Block 3):**
  - F12 May 22 — 20 V3 mass simulators + 2 modified V2 (~37,500 kg) — ship OK, booster crashed into Gulf at 1,450 km/h; first Block 3, first launch from Starbase OLP-2
  - F13 Jul 24 — 20 Starlink V3 (~34,000 kg) — success
  - F14 Sep 28 — 26 Starlink V3 (~44,000 kg) — success; **first orbital deployment of operational payload**
- F15 NET Oct 19 (ship catch planned).
- Gap between F11 (Oct 2025) and F12 (May 2026): **7 months** without a flight.

## Derived (our arithmetic — formula shown)
- Max demonstrated payload: **~44 t** (F14) vs the **200 t** per flight assumed by Google's and SpaceX's economics → **22%** of the assumed capacity.
- 2026 run-rate: 3 flights in 9 months = **4/yr**; 2025: 5/yr. Google's 180/yr = **36–45×** current cadence. Tonnage to orbit in 2026 to date ≈ 37.5 + 34 + 44 = **~115 t** vs the **37,000 t/yr** Google's curve needs.
- Success rate 2025–26: 5 of 8 flights nominal (**62%**).
