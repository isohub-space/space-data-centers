# Journal of Space Commerce — Space data center startup Orbital taps German firm Reflex Aerospace to build its satellites

## Reported (what the source says)
- **Orbital** (Los Angeles; CEO **Euwyn Poon**) signs development agreement with **Reflex Aerospace**
  (Berlin/Munich; CEO **Walter Ballheimer**) — Reflex becomes **system integrator and exclusive
  platform provider** for Orbital's compute satellites; Orbital is Reflex's **first US customer**.
- Orbital's plan: constellation of **up to 100,000 satellites**; **~250 kW peak** each; heat rejection
  "hundreds of kW continuously"; solar arrays larger than any current commercial satellite.
- No funding, launch partner, timeline or customers in the article. *(DCD headline, not opened:
  "California startup Orbital joins space data center craze with **$5M pre-seed**". Light Reading
  2026-06-10 lists "Orbital startup" among SpaceX's competitors.)*
- Poon: "Reflex is exactly the partner we need to build data centers in orbit." Ballheimer: "Orbital
  is pursuing an ambitious vision that requires spacecraft engineered specifically for this
  demanding mission."
