# UKSA + ESA launch €7.26 M InCubed call (16 Jun – 21 Aug 2026): €0.3–4 M per project, up to 80% co-funding for SMEs

> Publication date approximated to the call opening date (16 Jun 2026); the article's own date was not shown.

## Reported (what the source says)
- Call size **€7.26 M**; per-project **€300 k – €4 M**; duration **up to 18 months**; opens **16 Jun 2026**, closes **21 Aug 2026**.
- Co-funding: **up to ~80% for SMEs**, **~50% for larger organisations**.
- Two-stage: idea pitch (commercial opportunity, market potential) → full proposal; two streams (**De-risking Cycle**, **Product Development Cycle**).
- Eligible: "full EO value chain, including satellite technologies, ground infrastructure and data-driven applications".
- (UK-specific call; InCubed itself is open to ESA member states that subscribe, which includes Denmark and Germany.)

## Primary (InCubed listing — adds)
- Edge/onboard projects already in the InCubed portfolio: **IngeniArs HPCEX** (Class-1 GPU edge module), **SkyLabs NANOhpc-obc**, **Planetek AI-eXpress**, **Ubotica Orbital AI** (">30 EO models onboard", "hundreds of thousands of inferences"), **Deimos Insight4EO**. Aggregate (search summary, not opened): **>180 activities, €130 M invested as of Dec 2025**.

## Derived (our arithmetic — formula shown)
- €130 M / 180 activities ≈ **€0.7 M average** ESA contribution per InCubed activity — small cheques, but at 80% co-funding an SME's own stake on a €1 M activity is **€200 k**.
- Three InCubed-funded onboard-AI product lines (Ubotica, Planetek, IngeniArs) + two OBCs (SkyLabs, Deimos) = InCubed is **the** European funding door for this product class.

## Relevance to the Entrant
- **Concrete funding path**: InCubed via the Danish (or German) ESA subscription, €0.3–4 M, 18 months, 80% co-funded — sized exactly for a first the Entrant in-orbit demo on a hosted payload.
- The portfolio shows ESA Φ-lab will fund **several competing** onboard-AI vendors; being Danish/Nordic (no InCubed onboard-AI project yet from DK) is a geographic-return advantage.
