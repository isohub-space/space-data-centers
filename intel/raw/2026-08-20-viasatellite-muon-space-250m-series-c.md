# Via Satellite — Muon Space raises $250M Series C (the "other" $250M ODC round of Aug 2026)

Aviation Week's 2026-08-21 headline "Orbital Data Center Startups Each Unveil $250M Venture
Rounds" refers to **Starcloud and Muon Space**. Muon is a satellite manufacturer/EO operator,
not a pure ODC company — Aviation Week's framing stretches the category.

## Reported (what the source says)
- **$250M Series C**, lead **Eclipse Capital**; new: Galvanize, **Google**, **Salesforce Ventures**,
  Wellington Management, I Squared Capital, Toyota's Woven Capital; existing: Radical, Congruent,
  Costanoa, Activate, ACME, ArcTern, Overlap. **Total raised > $386M.**
- *(Satnews 2026-08-20, citing Bloomberg: valuation **$1.5B**; founded 2021; San Jose plant to
  make **up to 500 smallsats/yr by 2027**; **100% mission success**; backlog **>50 satellites**.)*
- **11 satellites launched** (7 in H1 2026); **50 in development**.
- Customers/missions: **FireSat** (Earth Fire Alliance), SNC/Hubble Network, Hydrosat.
- **Condor platform** debuted **June 2026**, designed for **orbital compute and networking** — no
  power class, GPU or customer disclosed.
- CEO Jonny Dyer: Mission Foundry lets customers "deploy complete constellation systems in months
  instead of years… space-based intelligence, communications, and **compute**."

## Derived (our arithmetic — formula shown)
- Google is now an investor in Muon **and** builds Suncatcher on Planet buses: Google is
  hedging across two EO-bus manufacturers for its orbital-compute path.

## Relevance to the Entrant
- Muon is the **bus supplier most likely to offer a compute-ready EO platform off the shelf**
  (Condor). For a small European EO-processing startup, Muon/Apex/Reflex are the "buy the bus"
  options; the question is whether a European bus (Reflex, OHB) can match Condor's compute hooks.
- The round shows investors funding **manufacturing capacity for EO constellations**, not GW AI —
  the realistic 2026–2029 money is in EO infrastructure, which is the Entrant's market.
