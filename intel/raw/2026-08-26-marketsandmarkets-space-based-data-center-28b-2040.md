# MarketsandMarkets — Space-based data center market worth $28.16B by 2040

## Reported (what the source says)
- Market **USD 0.11B in 2026 → USD 28.16B in 2040**, stated CAGR **18.3%** (2026–2040). Data period 2021–2040.
- Segments: payload (storage vs processing — processing fastest CAGR); power (**≤100 kW** largest share in 2026; **≥1 MW** fastest at **32.6% CAGR**); application (processing & analytics leads); end user (government & defence fastest); region (**Europe fastest at 43.6% CAGR**, North America second-largest).
- Drivers: strain on terrestrial infrastructure, AI demand, improving launch economics.
- Players named: Starcloud, Axiom Space, Kepler Communications, Cowboy Space Corporation, SpaceX.
- Other vendors (search snippets, not opened): Fortune Business Insights $1.44B (2026) → $3.81B (2034), 12.96% CAGR; Globe Market Research $1.9B (2026) → $8.4B (2035); IGTPS $1.70B (2026) → $6.62B (2034). Base-year estimates differ by **>15×** across vendors.

## Derived (our arithmetic — formula shown)
- CAGR check: 28.16 / 0.11 = 256× over 14 years → 256^(1/14) = **48.6% CAGR**, not 18.3%. The stated 18.3% applied to 0.11B gives 0.11 × 1.183^14 ≈ **$1.2B** in 2040. The headline endpoints and the headline CAGR are mutually inconsistent; one of them is a different base year or segment.
- ≤100 kW being the largest 2026 segment is consistent with the market being edge/hosted payloads (the Entrant class), not data centres.

## Relevance to the Entrant
- The only segment that exists in 2026 is **≤100 kW** payloads doing "processing & analytics" with government/defence as the fastest-growing buyer — i.e. The Entrant's addressable market as the analysts define it.
