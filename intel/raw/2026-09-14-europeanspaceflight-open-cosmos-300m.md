# Open Cosmos raises €300 M — the Φsat-2 / CogniSAT-6 builder becomes Europe's best-funded small-sat operator

## Reported (what the source says)
- **€300 M** announced **14 Sep 2026**; previous round **$50 M Series B (Sep 2023)**.
- Investors: **Lightrock, ETF Partners, Entrepreneurs First, Institut Català de Finances**, plus two unnamed international pension funds.
- **Four European manufacturing sites**, combined capacity **one satellite per day**; **>$370 M** of customer contracts secured over the past 3.5 years.
- Funds go to **OpenConstellation** (EO network), **ConnectedCosmos** (comms constellation) and **DataCosmos** (satellite-intelligence platform). Valuation not in this article (search summaries: ~$2 B, >$390 M total raised).

## Derived (our arithmetic — formula shown)
- €300 M is **~27× Ubotica's Series A** and **2× D-Orbit's Series C** — Open Cosmos is the European platform that can afford to internalise onboard AI (it built Φsat-2 and CogniSAT-6 and ran the JPL Dynamic Targeting demo).
- "One satellite per day" capacity vs Unibap's ~100 computers/yr: the bus side of Europe now out-produces the edge-compute side **~3.6×** — compute supply, not buses, is the constraint if every satellite is to carry AI.

## Relevance to the Entrant
- Open Cosmos is simultaneously the **best partner** (it manufactures the satellites the Entrant's software would fly on, and it already hosts third-party AI) and the **biggest risk** (DataCosmos could become the "insight layer" itself).
- A shared-constellation model (OpenConstellation) is the natural **host for a federated the Entrant compute node** — and it is where ESA's EOCognitiveLab "federation" language will likely land.
