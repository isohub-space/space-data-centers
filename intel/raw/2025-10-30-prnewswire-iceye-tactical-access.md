# ICEYE launches Tactical Access: guaranteed SAR tasking, sub-hour delivery for defence

## Reported (what the source says)
- **Tactical Access**: reserved imaging capacity with guaranteed short-notice availability for "national security customers" and "imagery power users".
- Delivery: **"sub-hour"** — **minutes with a customer ground station, hours via cloud** delivery.
- Gen-4 SAR: **up to 16 cm** resolution; Scan Wide **200 × 300 km**; electronic beam steering captures "dozens of high-resolution scenes in only minutes".
- Option: **dedicated ground station with ICEYE Edge processor** for direct downlink and **sovereign in-country processing**; data custody stays with the customer.
- John Cartwright (SVP Data Product): "breaking the paradigm of the traditional imagery planning and delivery model."
- No pricing, fleet size or customer count.

## Primary (where the underlying document differs or adds)
- Web-search summaries (not opened) of ICEYE's latency blog: a **15-minute latency target set in 2019**; processing now **under 5 minutes** for one- and two-look Spot products; vessel detection delivered in **<15 minutes** via KSAT; Sep 2025 **ISR Cell** deployable ground unit for field tasking/downlink/AI analysis.

## Derived (our arithmetic — formula shown)
- ICEYE's architecture puts the "edge" **on the ground next to the antenna**, not in orbit: minutes-latency is achieved with direct downlink + local processor. The in-orbit case must beat "minutes after a pass" — i.e. it only wins when **no pass is available** (mid-ocean, Arctic, denied areas) or when **cross-cueing other satellites** before the pass.

## Relevance to the Entrant
- The best-funded European EO operator with defence customers has chosen **ground-edge, not orbit-edge**, for latency. The Entrant's pitch must articulate why orbit beats a $-cheap ground station: pass gaps, relay-based delivery, autonomy/tip-and-cue, sovereignty of processing *in* the asset.
- "Sovereign in-country processing" is already a sold feature — the Entrant's sovereignty angle must be sharper (processing never leaves the European asset / relay).
