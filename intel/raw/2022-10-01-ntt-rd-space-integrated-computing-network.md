# NTT R&D — Overview of the Space Integrated Computing Network (NTT × SKY Perfect JSAT "space data center")

## Reported (what the source says)
- **Space Compass Corporation** established **2022-07-20** as a 50/50 JV of **NTT** and **SKY Perfect
  JSAT** (stake split per trade press, not in this page).
- Architecture: three layers — **HAPS** (stratosphere), **LEO**, **GEO** — connected by **optical
  wireless** links (IOWN) to form an integrated constellation for **distributed computing**.
- Three business projects: (1) **Space datacenter project** — optical data relay first; (2) Space RAN
  (B5G/6G); (3) Space sensing (LEO MIMO for IoT).
- Timeline: optical data relay service **FY2024**; HAPS low-latency comms in Japan **FY2025**; Osaka
  Expo optical demo **2025**; JAXA MIMO demo 2022.
- Optical GEO relay **~10× faster** than current radio services.
- **No power, compute or storage figures**; the "space data center" is defined here as **relay +
  distributed processing**, with EO imagery processed in orbit so only useful data is downlinked
  (that framing comes from DCD/trade press, which quoted a **2025 launch / 2026 operations** plan —
  DCD page 403, not verified).

## Primary (where the underlying document differs or adds)
- This is the primary. It is notably **less specific** than the 2022 press framing ("satellites
  launched 2025, operating 2026"); by 2026 Space Compass's public work is **optical relay** (JAXA
