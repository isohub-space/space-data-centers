# Orbital (US) inks deal with Germany's Reflex Aerospace to build AI data centres in space

## Reported (what the source says)
- **Orbital** (Southern California; founder/CEO **Euwyn Poon**) plans a **100,000-satellite** solar-powered AI compute constellation; first prototype launch **2027**; **$5 M pre-seed** led by **Andreessen Horowitz** (Jun 2026).
- **Reflex Aerospace** (Berlin; founded 2021; CEO **Walter Ballheimer**) designs the satellite bus; Reflex has raised **€59 M** to date including a **€50 M Series A (Nov 2025)** — "the largest Series A to go into a new space tech startup in Europe" at the time; one satellite launched (2024) for a customer under NDA.
- Context: ~18,000 satellites in orbit (vs 2,200 in 2019); Germany's **€35 B** space commitment cited.

## Derived (our arithmetic — formula shown)
- 100,000 satellites vs Starlink's ~7,000 flying — the constellation claim is **~14× Starlink**, on a $5 M pre-seed; treat as a vision statement, not a plan.
- Reflex's €59 M is **~4× Ubotica's total** and ~0.4× D-Orbit's Series C — the German bus maker is the best-capitalised potential *manufacturing partner* for a small orbital-compute startup in the DACH region.

## Relevance to the Entrant
- Confirms the **US-vision / German-hardware pattern**: US startups raise on the narrative, European firms supply the bus. The Entrant could be the *European* narrative owner for the EO-insight niche rather than the bus supplier.
- Reflex (Berlin) and Mynaric/Rocket Lab (Munich) make Germany the natural hardware hinterland for a Danish/German the Entrant.
