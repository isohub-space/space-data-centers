# Third AI-eXpress satellite (AIX-1+) in orbit — Italian ESA-InCubed edge-intelligence constellation complete

## Reported (what the source says)
- **AIX-1+** launched **28 Nov 2025** on Falcon 9 **Transporter-15** (SLC-4E, Vandenberg) into a **520 km SSO**; deployed ~1 h after launch. Completes the first generation of **three** satellites (**AIX-1, AIX-1p, AIX-1+**).
- Lead: **Planetek Italia**; partners **AIKO** (Turin, onboard autonomy software) and **D-Orbit** ("In Orbit Now" services, **SpaceCloud architecture** integration); **co-funded by ESA InCubed** (Φ-lab-managed EO programme).
- Compute: **Microchip PolarFire SoC** validated for radiation-tolerant AI-capable onboard computing; **Microchip VectorBlox** acceleration IP being characterised.
- Early adopters / tenants: **Eni** (low-latency AI-aided onboard pipelines), **S&T, VAKE, NOSA** (REMIS intelligent monitoring), **Ubotica** (NN optimisation, edge-AI portability), **IBM** (**TerraMind.tiny** foundation model deployed onboard), **Microchip**.
- Claims: "near-zero data latency through direct onboard processing", real-time analytics.
- Quote: Giovanni Sylos Labini (CEO): "With this third flight of the AIX-1+ satellite we are going to demonstrate the full capacity".

## Primary (ESA InCubed listing — adds)
- InCubed describes AIX as a "**hybrid edge/cloud architecture** integrating AI, blockchain and modular application deployment". Other InCubed edge projects named on the same page: **IngeniArs HPCEX** (Class-1-qualified edge module with space-qualified GPU), **SkyLabs NANOhpc-obc**, **Deimos Insight4EO**. Aggregate InCubed figures (web summary, not opened): >180 activities, **€130 M** invested as of Dec 2025.

## Derived (our arithmetic — formula shown)
- Three satellites in ~2 years with ESA co-funding at InCubed's typical **50–80%** co-funding share (see [[2026-06-16-innovationnewsnetwork-incubed-7-26m-call]]) — a national SME can get an orbital edge testbed flying for a few €M of own money.
- A **multi-tenant** satellite (Eni, IBM, Ubotica, S&T…) is already the operating model — AI-eXpress is effectively a small "in-orbit data centre for EO" marketed as an app-hosting platform.

## Relevance to the Entrant
- **Direct Italian precedent for the Entrant's concept**, already in orbit with ESA money, an oil major (Eni) and IBM as tenants. The Entrant's differentiation must be scale/latency (relay-connected, multi-satellite) or geography (Nordic/German sovereign), not the idea itself.
- Shows the **ESA InCubed route** works for this exact product class — and that Italy (Planetek, AIKO, D-Orbit, IngeniArs, ASI/IRIDE) is the most crowded European cluster.
