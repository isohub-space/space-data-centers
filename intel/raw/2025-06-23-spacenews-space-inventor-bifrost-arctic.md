# Space Inventor launches BIFROST — first Danish Arctic surveillance satellite, with onboard AI from Unibap

## Reported (what the source says)
- **BIFROST** launched **23 Jun 2025** on Falcon 9 **Transporter-14** (Vandenberg); **~50 kg** microsatellite, **5 m** wingspan; built on Space Inventor's (Aalborg) platform after **7 years** of development.
- Partners: **Terma**, **Gatehouse SatCom**, **DTU**, **Unibap** (Sweden; onboard AI computer).
- Funding: **DALO** (Danish Defence Acquisition and Logistics Organisation) and **FMV** (Swedish Defence Materiel Administration).
- Capabilities: optical + multispectral IR imaging, **AI-supported surveillance and target identification onboard**, **inter-satellite communication**, **real-time event reporting**; mission: monitor Arctic maritime activity (Greenland, Arctic, North Atlantic).
- Vision: **20 satellites** for 24-hour Arctic coverage.

## Derived (our arithmetic — formula shown)
- The Arctic is the canonical **pass-gap-free but ground-station-poor** region: polar orbits give frequent overflights, but Danish/Greenlandic ground segment is thin and weather is poor for optical DTE — the textbook case where onboard detection + ISL/relay event reporting beats raw downlink.

## Relevance to the Entrant
- **The Danish state has already bought onboard-AI event reporting for the Arctic** — the first domestic reference customer and use case for a Danish orbital-compute startup is defined and funded (DALO/FMV).
- The Danish supply chain is also defined: **Space Inventor** (bus), **Terma** (defence prime), **Gatehouse** (comms), **DTU** — the Entrant would slot in as the processing/insight layer or federate with BIFROST-class assets.
