# ESA EOCognitiveLab Info Day (29 Sep 2026, ESRIN): Φsat series + 3CS4EO become a two-satellite cooperative mission

## Reported (what the source says)
- Info Day **29 Sep 2026, 09:30–16:00, ESA-ESRIN Frascati**.
- **EOCognitiveLab** "brings together the evolution of the **Φsat mission series** and ESA's work on **Cognitive Cloud Computing in Space for Earth Observation (3CS4EO)**".
- Goal: demonstrate AI-enabled, **cooperative EO across multiple heterogeneous satellites** — event-based acquisition replanning, coordinated acquisitions, **onboard processing and inter-satellite-link information exchange**, distributed processing, cooperative sensing, **federated learning**.
- Architecture: a **core two-satellite constellation**, "an open constellation concept" that can be expanded by **federating additional assets**; advanced onboard computing and **open ISL interfaces**.
- Phases: Mission Consolidation Phase → Mission Implementation Phase (design through in-orbit commissioning). Operations are out of scope of the activity.
- Budget, ITT dates and Φsat-3 naming not on the page.

## Primary (where the underlying document differs or adds)
- Web-search summaries (not opened): ESA frames **3CS** as "capitalizing on high-performance AI accelerator chips directly onboard satellites"; **OPS-SAT VOLT** (ARTES ScyLight, optical/quantum comms testbed, reconfigurable payloads incl. FPGAs) planned for launch **2026**, with KP Labs involved.

## Relevance to the Entrant
- This is the **single most relevant ESA procurement** for the Entrant: an *open, federated* constellation with open ISL interfaces explicitly invites third-party assets to join. A Danish startup can position as a **federated compute node / service provider** rather than bidding to build the two core satellites (which Open Cosmos, OHB, Thales, etc. will chase).
- ESA's language ("cognitive cloud computing in space") is the institutional label for the Entrant's concept — align terminology in any ESA proposal.
- Denmark's ESA subscription and the Info Day date (just past) mean the ITT is imminent — timing matters.
