# D-Orbit announces on-orbit edge-computing collaboration with SkyServe STORM

## Reported (what the source says)
- D-Orbit (Italy) hosts **SkyServe's STORM** edge platform aboard **ION SCV004 "Elysian Eleonora"**, with a "more performant ION" planned for **2025**; mission name **"Mission Matterhorn"**.
- STORM processes EO imagery into inferences in orbit: **smart discard, tasking, compression, deep learning**; pitched to geospatial analytics companies wanting to deploy AI models on satellites.
- Prior D-Orbit edge heritage referenced: collaboration with **Unibap** (iX5) on earlier ION missions.
- Quotes: Vinay Simha (SkyServe CEO): "perform complex processing tasks in orbit, making space data more accessible for clients"; D-Orbit BD head: aligns with "our mission to provide comprehensive in-orbit services".
- No latency, data-volume, pricing or customer-count figures.

## Relevance to the Entrant
- D-Orbit is the **closest European analogue to "an orbital compute host you can rent"**: it already sells hosted compute slots on a carrier it flies several times a year (16 missions by Sep 2024, 7 planned for 2025). The Entrant would either ride on ION as a customer or compete with it as a host.
- SkyServe (India) shows the **software-only entry**: no own satellite, deploy models on someone else's compute. That is the low-capex path a small team can take before owning hardware.
