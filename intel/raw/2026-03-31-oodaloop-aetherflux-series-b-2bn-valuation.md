# OODAloop/DCD — Aetherflux reportedly raising Series B at $2B valuation (WSJ)

## Reported (what the source says)
- Aetherflux targeting **$250–300M Series B at $2B valuation**, led by existing investor **Index
  Ventures** (WSJ, unnamed sources).
- Prior: **$50M Series A** (Index + Interlagos) plus an **additional $30M**; Series A investors
  Breakthrough Energy Ventures, a16z, NEA, Jared Leto.
- Product: "high performance AI compute data centers in orbit" leveraging space solar, constellation
  branded "**Galactic Brain**".
- **First commercial data center node: Q1 2027.**

**Outcome (cross-ref):** closed **2026-05-11 as $275M at $2B**, company renamed **Cowboy Space**
([[intel/raw/2026-05-11-techcrunch-cowboy-space-275m-series-b]]). The "Galactic Brain" brand did
not survive; the Q1 2027 "first node" became a **Reason-2 demo with H200 GPUs in H1 2027**
([[intel/raw/2026-10-01-payload-transporter-18-odc-building-blocks]]) with the first 1 MW
production node not before **end 2028**.
