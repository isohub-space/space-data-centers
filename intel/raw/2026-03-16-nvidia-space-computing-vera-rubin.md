# NVIDIA — "NVIDIA Launches Space Computing, Rocketing AI Into Orbit" (GTC 2026)

## Reported (what the source says)
- **Vera Rubin Space Module**: "up to **25×** more AI compute for space-based inferencing" than an H100; SWaP-constrained design; integrated CPU-GPU with high-bandwidth interconnect; availability "at a later date" (no date).
- Available today for space: **IGX Thor, Jetson Orin, RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell**.
- Partners named: **Aetherflux** (power & compute), **Axiom Space**, **Kepler Communications**, **Planet Labs PBC**, **Sophia Space** (passively cooled hosted compute), **Starcloud**.
- Jensen Huang: *"Space computing, the final frontier, has arrived… intelligence must live wherever data is generated."* Elsewhere (search snippet, CNBC): *"In space there's no conduction, there's no convection, there's just radiation, and so we have to figure out how to cool these systems."*
- No radiation-hardening specification published for the Rubin module.
