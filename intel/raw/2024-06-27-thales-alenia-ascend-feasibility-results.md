# Thales Alenia Space — results of the ASCEND feasibility study on space data centres

## Reported (what the source says)
- ASCEND = *Advanced Space Cloud for European Net zero emission and Data sovereignty*; **Horizon Europe**-funded, consortium coordinated by Thales Alenia Space: Carbone 4, VITO (environment); Orange Business, CloudFerro, HPE (cloud); ArianeGroup (launch); DLR, Airbus Defence & Space (orbital systems).
- Purpose: compare environmental impact of space vs Earth data centres and validate technical feasibility.
- Headline conclusions:
  - Needs a launcher **10× less emissive over its lifecycle** than today's to deliver a net CO₂ benefit.
  - **No water** needed for cooling.
  - Target **1 GW deployed before 2050**; context figure of **23 GW** estimated European data-centre capacity by 2030.
  - "Economic viability confirmed" with "a return on investment of several billion euros between now and 2050."
  - Modular infrastructure **assembled in orbit robotically**; relies on the **EROSS IOD** robotics demo, first mission 2026.
- Christophe Valorge (CTO): results "confirm space data centers could transform European digital landscape."
- Next step: consolidate and optimise the study.

## Primary (where the underlying document differs or adds)
- Dec 2023 project page: goal of **10 TWh** capacity to cut European data-centre energy use by 10%; **10 MW per space data centre** block needing **~35,000 m² of solar panels** (vs ISS 7,500 m²); baseline **20 Mt CO₂/yr** from European terrestrial data centres (2023–2030); 11 partners since January 2023.
- The full study report is not public; only the press summary. No $/kg assumption, no capex figure is given.

## Derived (our arithmetic — formula shown)
- 1 GW ÷ 10 MW per block = **100 blocks**, each ~35,000 m² PV → **3.5 km²** of solar array for the 2050 target.
- "Several billion euros ROI by 2050" against a 1 GW system whose launch mass alone (at 34–59 kg/kW, Turyshev) is 34,000–59,000 t is not reconcilable without the study's cost model — treat as a claim, not a figure.

## Relevance to the Entrant
- ASCEND is the European sovereign narrative the Entrant can attach to: "net-zero + data sovereignty" is the EU framing, and the ESA Space Cloud follow-on (Edge Aerospace study, 2026) shows ESA still funding the question rather than the answer.
- The 10× cleaner launcher precondition means Europe's own path is gated on Ariane successors — a reason for the Entrant to be launcher-agnostic and small.
