# Earth-observation operators push to deliver satellite images within minutes

- Lede (the only readable paragraph): at **Vantor**'s (ex-Maxar) sales kick-off in **Jan 2026**, a **WorldView Legion** satellite imaged the California venue; the **30 cm** image appeared on the Vantor Hub portal **13 minutes** later — presented as the new benchmark for tasking-to-delivery latency.
