# Space Compass — selected for JAXA "Space Strategy Fund" for optical data-relay services

## Reported (what the source says)
- Space Compass (NTT × SKY Perfect JSAT JV) selected under JAXA's Space Strategy Fund programme
  "Research, Development, and Demonstration toward the Realization of Data Relay Services Utilizing
  Satellite Optical Communications".
- **Maximum grant ¥23.5 billion**; project period **through March 2031**; stage-gated.
- Scope: **GEO optical relay satellite**; multi-orbit orchestration for end-to-end paths; dynamic
  network control across orbital planes.
- Co-CEO Hiromi Komatsu: optical relay "enable[s] **real-time Earth observation** by overcoming
  data-latency challenges… national security and disaster response."
- **No in-orbit compute / space data center element** in this grant.

## Relevance to the Entrant
- Japan's state answer to "time-to-insight for EO" is **faster downlink** (GEO optical relay), not
  in-orbit processing. The Entrant's argument must show why processing in orbit still wins when relay
  latency falls — i.e. **bandwidth cost and data volume**, not latency alone.
- A European equivalent (ESA HydRON / EDRS successors) is the public-funding channel to watch;
  Space Compass–ESA signed a HydRON interoperability MoI in March 2025 (search snippet, not opened).
