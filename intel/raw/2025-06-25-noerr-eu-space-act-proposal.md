# EU Commission proposes the EU Space Act (25 June 2025)

## Reported (what the source says)
- Proposal published **25 Jun 2025**, >100 pages plus annexes; aims to replace fragmented national regimes with a **single EU authorisation framework**.
- Scope: "space companies" and **"all entities providing space-related services, including those dealing with space-based data"** — i.e. data/processing service providers are in scope, not only satellite operators.
- Authorisation by **national authorities**; whole **constellations can be approved uniformly** rather than satellite-by-satellite.
- Cybersecurity obligations: risk-management systems, access rights, physical resilience, cryptography, crisis communication.
- Environmental: a **common assessment procedure for environmental impacts** of space activities.
- Safety/traffic: manoeuvrability requirements, orbital traffic rules, registration, end-of-life disposal.
- Web-search summaries of other law-firm notes (not opened): three pillars **safety, resilience, sustainability**; applies to **third-country operators serving the EU**; stated goal to ease cross-border activity for **start-ups and SMEs**; rules on **in-space operations and services** to be specified via delegated acts. Application timing (often cited as 2030) not confirmed from an opened source.

## Relevance to the Entrant
- An orbital-compute service that **hosts third-party data** would fall under the Act's cybersecurity and "space-based data services" scope — compliance design (encryption, access control, incident reporting) becomes a product requirement, and also a **sovereignty selling point** vs US hosts.
- Uniform constellation authorisation and SME provisions lower the regulatory cost of a small European constellation — mildly positive for a Danish/German startup.
