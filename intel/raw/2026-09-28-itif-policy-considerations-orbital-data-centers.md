# ITIF — "Policy Considerations for Orbital Data Centers"

## Reported (what the source says)
- US regulatory touchpoints: FCC "Spectrum Abundance for Weird Space Stuff" proceeding (TT&C allocations); proposed **SAT Streamlining Act**; FCC satellite/earth-station licensing modernisation; **NOAA** remote-sensing licences under 15 CFR Part 960; export controls 15 CFR 730–774; **Space Policy Directive-5** cybersecurity principles; **ORBITS Act** (active debris remediation); NOAA **TraCSS** space-traffic coordination.
- Engineering: thermal management is "the most significant engineering constraint"; radiation faults; no servicing; hardware obsolescence.
- Positioning: best suited to **Earth observation, remote sensing, spacecraft autonomy and space-native processing**; "complementary infrastructure, not replacement."
- Recommendations: spectrum clarity, streamlined FCC/NOAA licensing, expanded launch infrastructure, debris mitigation & traffic coordination, cybersecurity expectations across space–ground, minimal regulatory friction.
- No cost figures or constellation numbers given.
