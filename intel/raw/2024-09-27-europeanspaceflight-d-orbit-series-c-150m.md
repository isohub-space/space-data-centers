# D-Orbit extends Series C to €150 M

## Reported (what the source says)
- Additional **€50 M** closed **27 Sep 2024**, taking the Series C to **€150 M** (initial **€100 M** in **Jan 2024**).
- Lead: **Marubeni Corporation** (holds exclusive distribution rights for D-Orbit in Japan; expanding to Southeast Asia).
- Recommitting investors: CDP Venture Capital, Seraphim Space Investment Trust, Indaco Venture Partners, Neva SGR, Primo Ventures. New: Avantgarde, Iberis Capital, **European Innovation Council Fund**, Terna Forward.
- Company: founded 2011, Italy; **16 successful missions** to date; **7 missions planned in 2025**.
- Stated use of funds: **"space cloud computing development"**, strategic acquisitions, in-orbit services (life extension, debris removal).
- No revenue or headcount.

## Derived (our arithmetic — formula shown)
- 16 missions over ~4 years of ION flights (first ION 2020) ≈ 4/yr, rising to 7/yr planned — i.e. roughly **one hosted-compute launch opportunity every ~7 weeks** from a single European carrier operator.

## Relevance to the Entrant
- The largest European venture round explicitly naming **space cloud computing** as a use of funds; D-Orbit is the capitalised incumbent in exactly the Entrant's segment.
- **EIC Fund participation** signals that EU public venture money is already backing orbital compute in Italy — a precedent for a Danish/German/Nordic applicant to the EIC Accelerator.
