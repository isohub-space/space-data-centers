# TechCrunch — Satlyt raises $8M seed to run AI on satellites ("Android of orbital computing")

## Reported (what the source says)

**Round**
- **$8M seed**, lead **Non Sibi Ventures** (Houston); ex-astronaut **Bernard Harris** involved. *(Search
  snippet of the press release adds TLCOM, Antler, Slauson & Co., Launch Africa Ventures, Enza
  Capital, Demos Capital, BAG Collective, Gaingels, Askya, AXIAN Investment; total "well past $10M"
  — not verified from an opened page.)*

**Company**
- Founder/CEO **Rama Afullo** (Kenyan-American; ex-Google cloud, ex-SpaceX Starlink 2024). CTO
  Nelson Psenjen; engineers Leina Moli, Junn Wangari. HQ **Sunnyvale, CA**; office **Nairobi**.
- Product: **software platform** to run AI models on *other operators'* satellites — "virtual
  orbital data center"; compares itself to VMware/Snowflake; positions as the open "Android" vs
  SpaceX's closed "iPhone" approach. Goal: present on **20% of satellites by 2030**.

**Missions**
- Launch **2026-10-02** on SpaceX rideshare (Transporter-18) alongside Google Suncatcher; hosted
  on a **TakeMe2Space** satellite. Customers on this mission: **NASA** (cloud protocols), **Stellerian**
  (space surveillance image processing), **TakeMe2Space** (multi-tenant demo).
- Two prior missions; on a **Momentus** spacecraft, running **Google DeepMind Gemma**, cut
  transmission size by **>60%**.

- A >60% downlink reduction on EO imagery is the one **measured time-to-insight/bandwidth KPI**

## Relevance to the Entrant
- **Direct competitor on the software layer**: Satlyt sells exactly "process EO data where it is,
  send down insights", hardware-agnostic, already flown three times, with NASA and a space-domain-
  awareness customer. The Entrant must differentiate on **EO-specific models / European sovereign
  customers / dedicated compute**, not on the abstract "edge AI on sats" story.
- Seed size ($8M) shows the software-only slice is **venture-fundable at seed** without launch
  risk — the hardware-light path is open to a European entrant.
- Multi-tenant hosting on TakeMe2Space / Momentus is the go-to-market template: fly on others'
  buses first.
