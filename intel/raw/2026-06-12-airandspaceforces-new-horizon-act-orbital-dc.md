# Air & Space Forces — "Senators Want Pentagon to Tap in to Commercial On-Orbit Data Centers"

## Reported (what the source says)
- **New Horizon Act**, bipartisan, Sen. **Ted Cruz** (R-TX) and Sen. **John Hickenlooper** (D-CO).
- Directs DoD to stand up a **pilot programme within one year** to: assess commercially available in-orbit cloud/data-processing hubs; integrate them with military data-transport satellites and ground systems; evaluate protection against kinetic, non-kinetic and cyber threats.
- Lead: **DIU**, with Space Force, **NRO** and **NGA**, under the existing **Hybrid Space Architecture** programme.
- FY2027 budget request: **$220M** for operational experimentation.
- Cruz: the bill "enables the Department of War to conduct operational testing on space-based data processing and storage, and will help to reduce latency, improve resilience, and enhance operational effectiveness."
- Companies cited as pursuing on-orbit compute: Google (Suncatcher), SpaceX, OpenAI, NVIDIA, Blue Origin, Starcloud.

## Primary (where the underlying document differs or adds)
- Universe Magazine / Reuters (2026-05-27): SpaceX awarded **$2.29B** by the US Space Force for the **Space Data Network (SDN) Backbone** — a secure high-speed comms layer for Golden Dome, prototype by **end-2027**; Space Force to pick additional vendors over the summer. The secondary headline calls these "orbital data centers"; the Reuters-sourced body describes a **data-transport** network, not compute. Treat as comms, not ODC.
