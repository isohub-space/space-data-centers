# Ramon.Space — raises $26M to fuel space computing infrastructure (rad-hard incumbent)

## Reported (what the source says)
- **$26M** round led by **Ingrasys (Foxconn subsidiary)** and Abu Dhabi's **Strategic Development
  Fund**; existing: Grove Ventures, Deep Insight, UMC Capital. *(Trade press, not opened: Series B;
  total raised **>$45M**.)*
- Products: **NuStream, NuPod, NuBox** — storage, processing, communication; **in-house
  radiation-hardened** technology, AI/ML processors; offices US + Israel.
- Heritage: **>50 deep-space and satellite missions, zero failures** (NASA, ESA cited by trade press).
- Strategic **global manufacturing agreement with Ingrasys** for Ramon.Space computing products.
- CEO Avi Shabtai: "Space-grade computing solutions have become an essential foundation for the
  evolution of the space economy."
- Positioning: satcom, remote sensing, autonomous robotics, exploration; commercial and defence.
  No "orbital data center" language in the 2023 release.

## Relevance to the Entrant
- Ramon.Space is the **rad-hard alternative** to the Nvidia COTS path: proven on 50 missions but
  at pre-2023 performance levels and with a Foxconn-manufactured, non-CUDA stack. For EO
  time-to-insight the trade is **reliability vs model performance per watt**; the market (Nvidia's
  six partners, Starcloud's H100) has voted COTS.

  a supplier option, not a competitor.
