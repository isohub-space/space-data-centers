# Payload — Aetherflux raises $50M Series A

## Reported (what the source says)
- **$50M Series A** led by **Index Ventures and Interlagos**; also Breakthrough Energy Ventures,
  a16z, NEA, Jared Leto. Founder **Baiju Bhatt** (Robinhood) had put in **$10M** personally.
- Business at the time: **space solar power** — collect solar energy in orbit, beam to ground via
  **infrared lasers**; value prop "energy where it's needed, day or night", remote military ops and
  disaster zones; **DoD funding** for a LEO power-transmission proof of concept.
- Bhatt: space solar "can give the US an asymmetric advantage… particularly in remote or
  contested Indo-Pacific regions."
- **No mention of data centers or compute** in the Series A messaging.

## Relevance to the Entrant
- Baseline for the pivot: within **~12 months** Aetherflux went from power-beaming to "AI compute
  in orbit" (Mar 2026) to rockets + 1 MW nodes (May 2026). The laser-beaming IP is now pitched as
  **optical data links** for ODCs — a capability a small EO-compute node would buy, not build.
